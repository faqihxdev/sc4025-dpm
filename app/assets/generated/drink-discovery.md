## Design plan and review

Keep Manrope and the existing layout. Keep house green #1e3932 for the original
drink circles, Starbucks green #00754a for controls, mint #d4e9e2 for selection, white #ffffff for
cards and line grey #dce1de for borders. Coffee brown, matcha green and berry pink
belong to the beverages, not new UI accent colours.

`[ four-drink circle ]  Help me choose / short chat invitation  >`

The illustration replaces only the chat glyph. The card's size, text alignment,
trailing-icon alignment and interaction stay consistent with the other three
options. Its trailing chevron matches the other three cards using the existing
Lucide icon family, size and stroke weight. Four
recognisable drinks provide variety without small decorations or new motion.
The discovery circle has a single light mint #d4e9e2 backdrop using the existing
light-green token. It replaces the multicolour patches so the beverages provide
the variety. The rest of the card retains the existing app palette. The backdrop is CSS, so it
does not require another image download or change the transparent source.
Review the actual thumbnail at 320px and 375px, not just the full-size artwork.

choose' option in a Starbucks-inspired mobile drink finder. Primary request: a
playful little world of drink possibilities, a lively, photorealistic four-drink
collage that remains clear when displayed at only 62 pixels. Subject: four

strawberry-pink iced refresher and a warm white ceramic cup of whipped-cream-topped

canvas and all important details fit inside a circular crop. Materials: realistic
condensation, ice, glass, milk and soft whipped cream, studio product photography,
crisp shapes with soft light, not cartoon or flat vector. Palette: coffee brown,
leafy matcha green, bright berry pink, warm white, designed to sit on the app's
dark forest green #1e3932 circle. Genuinely transparent background around the
group. No floor, no broad cast shadow, no writing, no logos, no Nutri-Grade labels,
no speech bubbles, no people, no watermarks, no tiny props or busy decorations.
The beverages should look delicious and fun, with clean silhouette separation at
